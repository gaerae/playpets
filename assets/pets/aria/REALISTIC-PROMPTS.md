# Realistic Aria artwork prompts and provenance

Generated with the built-in image generation/editing tool. These are original fictional adult companion images; no personal photos were used. All source batches are 1536 × 1024 PNGs, four columns by two rows, with true alpha. The unchanged v2 production format is 1536 × 2288 with eight columns and eleven rows. Spring and autumn share the original outfit.

The root-approved idle anchor is the identity and proportion authority. Packing uses `scripts/pack-companion-batches.mjs` with a 176px idle silhouette, a shared scale for every action in each sheet, and strict clipping guards. No local repainting, geometry editing or frame substitutions were used. Working shows focused standing book reading: repeated seated attempts enlarged the head, and this semantic alternative was explicitly authorized. Walking has visible heel and passing-pose changes but limited leg alternation; it is a compact desktop stroll rather than a naturalistic gait. The direct-down gaze has lowered lids with a bowed head; the remaining gaze frames turn toward their specified directions.

Rejected 07/09/10 generations and their exact repair prompts remain beside the accepted spring sources. Those files are not packed. Earlier prompts containing the requested seated laptop were rejected in favor of the accepted standing book row below.
