# Places: Woodland and Window studio

Consolidated on 2026-10-13 from the design work of 2026-10-11 and 2026-10-12.

PlayPets has two places: Woodland, the outdoor yard it starts in, and Window studio, a
calm room above a city to keep open while working. Both hold the same friends,
conversations, games, garden and collection; only where they stand changes. A place is
more than a picture: its floor, the activities it offers, its weather and its sound have
to agree with what people see. To add one, follow [Add a place for
friends](../how-to/add-a-world.md).

## One registry

`src/content/worlds.json` lists every place. An entry has a stable `id`, a `label` and
an `about` key from the page string tables, an optional `chatter` key for idle lines,
optional `art` per time of day, `authoredLighting`, optional `windowPanes`, a `fit`, its
`geometry` and its `capabilities`.

`src/yard/worlds.js` reads the registry. `worldFor` sends an unknown or removed id back
to `woodland`, so changing an id later returns saved choices to Woodland. `worldAllows`
treats a capability as on unless the entry sets it to `false`. `setupSky` in
`src/yard/sky.js` builds the Place select in the scene menu from the registry and saves
the choice with `remember('world')` (local storage), beside the time, weather and season
choices.

## Capabilities

A capability belongs to a place, not to an activity's code. Each flag is checked where
its work happens:

| Capability | Woodland | Studio | What it turns on |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sky` | on | off | Painted sky, sun, moon, clouds and stars (`easel.js`, `scenery.js`); weather remarks also need it (`remarks.js`) |
| `weather` | on | on, behind the panes | Rain and snow on the weather canvas and the land (`scenery.js`, `environment.js`) |
| `wildlife` | on | off | Birds, bugs to catch and fireflies (`scenery.js`) |
| `fishing` | on | off | The lake's water and casting (`catch.js`, `environmentLake`) |
| `seasonalEffects` | on | off | Falling leaves and petals, festival cards and lights (`scenery.js`, `festival.js`, `environment.js`) |
| `ambient` | on | off | The woodland sound mix (`soundmix.js`); activity cues still play |

The studio's idle lines come from its `chatter` key (`world.studio.lines`) instead of
the outdoor time and weather lines, so friends do not talk about stars or snow falling
indoors. A third place gets its own `about` and `chatter` keys rather than inheriting
the studio's.

## Floor geometry and fit

`geometry.fieldTop` is the back edge of the walkable floor as a fraction of the image
height: 0.66 in Woodland and 0.55 in the studio. `src/yard/field.js` uses it when the
place's picture is shown and the composition's own field when the painted fallback is.

`fit: "cover-floor"` keeps the picture's proportions: `environmentFrame` in
`src/yard/environment.js` scales the image to cover the yard and lines its floor up with
the yard's. `cover-bottom` aligns the bottom edge instead. The decision was to crop,
never stretch: very wide mini strips lose the top of the window or the hills and show
more floor, and narrow windows lose the sides. Woodland was stretched in wide strips
before this and now uses the same transform.

Everything placed over the picture uses that one frame: the lake (`environmentLake`,
from `geometry.lake` with `from`, `to`, `center` and `radius`), the window panes and the
seasonal lights. Fishing in `src/yard/catch.js` asks for the lake again when the size,
the place or the picture-or-fallback mode changes, so a cast never aims at water that
moved. A place without fishing returns no lake, and both manual and automatic fishing
check the capability.

## Authored lighting

Woodland's land is one of four transparent seasonal layers (`green`, `spring`, `autumn`,
`winter` in `src/art/environments/`), drawn over the live sky. `environmentLook` tints
it for each time of day and for rain.

The studio has a picture for each time of day: `studio-modern-city-morning`, `-day`,
`-evening` and `-night`. A colour filter cannot turn a midday city into a sunset or a
lit night skyline, so each time was painted on its own from the same camera, and
`"authoredLighting": true` tells `environmentLook` to add no filter or tint. A place
with pictures but without authored lighting gets a gentle filter for each time of day.
The room is ivory stone with slim graphite frames, a cream chair and restrained metal
furniture, with an open floor where the friends stand.

## Weather behind window panes

The studio enables `weather` and lists three `windowPanes`, normalised rectangles over
the glass. `weatherPanes` moves them through the same frame as the picture, so rain,
snow and overcast shading are clipped to the glass that is still visible. The floor
stays dry and gets no ripples, no second whole-room tint is added, and when cropping
hides every pane the weather canvas does no work at all. Reduced motion draws still
weather.

## What stays outdoors

Fishing, wildlife, falling leaves and petals, festival effects and cards, the sky and
the forest sound stay in Woodland. The studio has no clickable water. A future indoor or
city sound needs its own mix: turning on `ambient` today selects the woodland one.

## Switching places

The `worldChanged` handler in `src/app.js` ends any game and fishing, releases every
friend from its trip, then enters the new place and lays out friends, the garden and
gifts again. Pending remarks are cancelled first, and a festival card that was not seen
does not use up its yearly announcement. Friends keep their identity, saved positions,
relationships, AI connections and collected items; none of that is stored per place.

## Painting and loading

The easel paints only when the scene or the size changes, or when a picture finishes its
first load (see [the performance standard](../standards/performance.md)). A place's
pictures load only when it is selected. While the next picture decodes, the current one
stays on the wall; a late image paints only if it still belongs to the active scene.
Crossing between places never fades the outdoor painting over the room. If a room
picture cannot load, the land is a plain warm surface rather than an outdoor hill.

## Artwork and sources

The selected studio prompts are in `src/art/environments/MODERN-STUDIO-PROMPTS.md`, and
the environment provenance in `src/art/environments/README.md`. The earlier studio
pictures (`studio.png`, `studio-night.png` and the four `studio-city-*` images of the
warm wooden room) are kept as sources in `assets/sources/environments/`; the app does
not ship them. Art direction for the scenery is in [the art direction
record](2026-10-11-art-direction.md).

## Places not built

These are proposals, not choices in the app:

- **Reading café:** indoor capabilities, a tiled or timber floor and a counter at the
  back; it needs its own sound rather than the forest mix.
- **City terrace:** outdoor sky and weather but no fishing or wildlife, a railing with
  depth, its own floor line and a city sound palette.
- **Quiet observatory:** a room with its own night view and lines.

Expose a place only when its art covers every time of day, its floor fits the whole cast
and the tools, and its capabilities, lines and sound are reviewed together.

`tests/window-weather.test.mjs`, `tests/world-remarks.test.mjs`,
`tests/world-festival.test.mjs` and `tests/environment.test.mjs` cover the registry, the
