# One hundred small discoveries

## Intent

Make the album a memorable 100-entry goal through ordinary nature, seasonal color
and playful everyday objects. No flags, national dress, political symbols,
religious objects or country-specific festivals are added.

## Collection

Retain every saved ID and its acquisition path. Add eight flowers, two per season,
four freshwater fish, four daytime insects and five common dug keepsakes.
The resulting books contain 16 flowers, 23 fish, 20 bugs, 18 gifts, six trees and
17 keepsakes. The six books total 100, with no new calendar-exclusive entries.

Seeds still come only from digging. Four flowers per planted season have equal
weights; growing time does not change. Fish and bugs keep the shared season,
time, weather and family-pool rules. New keepsakes have seasonal and place
preferences, remain possible outside those preferences, and do not enter the
uncommon reward tier. The existing rolling rare-find interval stays unchanged.

## Presentation and compatibility

Use new transparent illustrated atlases with reviewed isolated crops, the existing
SVG wrapper tool and four-language names. Existing artwork and IDs stay intact.
Show habitat clues and qualitative rarity, never exact probability figures or a
control for revealing them. Internal probability helpers remain for balance tests.
The web guide reads the same catalogue as the app. External pets are unaffected.

## Completion contract

Every entry has a reachable acquisition path. Each season exposes four flowers;
new fish have a practical favored context; common discoveries remain available
while uncommon finds are waiting. Expanding ordinary pools does not add rare
calendar gates, change daily gifts or let the full album complete in one session.
Review decoded art, transparent gutters, compact and wide album layouts, the
native app, locale names and persisted collection behavior before finishing.
