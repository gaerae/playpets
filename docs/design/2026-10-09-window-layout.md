# Where things live in the window (2026-10-09)

## Why

Features were added one at a time (scenery pickers, a greet button, games, a garden, gifts, fishing), each finding a spot of its own. The window had four stacked frames (top bar, card border, caption bar, toolbar) around a scene that should be the point of the app, and the same switch was named differently in the top bar, the tray and the menu bar. This settles one place for each kind of thing and one set of words.

## Principles

- **The scene is the window.** The painting fills the yard's frame; everything else floats over it as small chips, as in games kept open all day (Animal Crossing, Spirit City).
- **Doing is in the scene; configuring is in the panel.** What a person does with friends (pat, talk, fish, catch, water, arrange gifts) happens on the yard itself or from the tools; what they set once lives in Settings.
- **One name per switch, shown as a switch.** Desktop mode is "Show on desktop" in the top bar, the tray and the menu bar alike; the top bar's button stays pressed while it is on instead of changing its words.
- **Group by purpose.** The tools are grouped as caring, growing and collecting, and playing; the panels as friends, inviting and (apart) settings.
- **Every place has a keyboard way in.** The panels and the two views have shortcuts on every system; on macOS they are also in the View menu.

## What changed

- 2026-10-10: Desktop mode and the mini view left the top bar for the yard's view controls; the Friend panel became a messenger layout, with what is done to the friend in its menu and the AI picker and group chat in the composer.
- 2026-10-31: The window can always be recovered from outside it. The top of the window shows Reset window size, minimize and close with the pointer or the keyboard, still with no title bar; the tray, the macOS Window menu, the scenery's right-click menu and Cmd/Ctrl+0 also reset it. A panel chosen from the right-click menu opens at the pointer.
- 2026-10-10 (later): The Friend tab's header shows the friend's relationship as a chip (a click opens it in Friend settings) and a closeness row (the level and a bar), in place of the friendship shown before; hearts on the name tag now stand for closeness.

- The caption bar and the toolbar became chips and a floating row over the scene; the two always-shown pickers became the scene label's menu.
- The greet button was removed (it did little anyone would miss); games moved into one menu.
- Desktop mode became a pressed switch with the tray's words; Settings became a gear; Settings gained a "The yard" group.
- The album became the one place for everything found, so the garden, the gifts and the catches read as one collection.
