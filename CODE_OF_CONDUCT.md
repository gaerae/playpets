# Code of Conduct

The PlayPets community follows the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Below is a summary; the original text is authoritative.

## Our pledge
We make a space where anyone can take part free from harassment, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Expected behavior
- Respect other people's opinions and experiences, and listen to differing viewpoints with an open mind.
- Give constructive feedback, and accept it gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologize, and learn from them.
- Think about what is best for the community as a whole, not just for individuals.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Applies to
This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and every public space where someone represents the community through an official account.

## Reporting
Report problem behavior to the maintainers privately. The project has no separate conduct inbox yet, so use the repository's private report form ([Security → Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/gaerae/playpets/security/advisories/new)) and start the title with "Conduct:". Only the maintainers can read it, and it is handled as a conduct report, not a vulnerability. If you would rather not use the form, open an issue that only asks for a private channel, without details. Every report is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's safety and privacy are protected.

## Enforcement
Maintainers can remove or edit comments, commits, code and issues that do not follow this code, and for repeated or serious cases they can restrict participation temporarily or permanently. The steps follow the Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines (correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
