# Guide

PlayPets is a shared yard for companions who talk to you and each other. Invite a starter or import a compatible community pet file, then choose a name, personality and relationship. Codex itself is not required.

Your first yard opens on a sunny spring day. Choose a season, time and weather, or switch them to Auto. Friends stroll, eat treats, play three games, find seeds and bring gifts. Grow flowers, fish and catch bugs for the album, and grow closer over time. Resize the window to keep the yard beside your work; desktop mode lets friends roam over other windows. Conversation needs a local model, a signed-in CLI or another AI connection. Coding-agent hooks are optional.

## What you need

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Operating system | macOS, Windows, Linux |
| AI | Optional. Inviting, playing and desktop mode all work without one |
| Coding agent | Optional. Works with Claude Code, Codex CLI, Gemini CLI, Cursor and tools that use MCP |
| Internet | Needed for downloads, community imports, updates and remote AI. Yard activities and a local Ollama model already downloaded to your computer can work offline |

The app is built with Tauri and uses the operating system's webview rather than bundling a full browser.

## Two words

- A **pet** is a character file. One folder with a `pet.json` and a sprite sheet is one pet. Pets in Codex's pet folder show up automatically, and you can also import pets from community sites or pick a folder on your computer.
- A **friend** is a pet you have invited into the yard. Invite the same pet several times and you get several friends, each with their own name, personality, size and AI.
