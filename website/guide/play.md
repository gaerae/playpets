# The yard and games

The yard changes with the time of day, the weather and the season, and it grows a little more each day. Friends dig up flower seeds and saplings and bring you gifts. You fish and catch bugs to fill the album, and your friends grow closer to you along the way.

Seasonal events come and go, the yard has its own sounds, and there are three games to play with your friends.

**The dock**

The scenery fills the window. The dock at the bottom has **Place & atmosphere**, **Play** and **Friends & settings**. Each one opens a single page split into sections, with no tabs or Back buttons inside, and only one yard panel is open at a time.

Automatic time follows your computer's clock without showing a clock.

**Play**

The Play panel is titled for the friend you've selected, such as **Play with Clover**, and has three sections:

| Section | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Find** | **Dig**, **Fish**, **Album** |
| **Garden** | **Plant a seed** (with how many seeds you have), **Plant a tree**, **Picnic** |
| **Group games** | The three games, **Let them chat** |

Let them chat needs two friends connected to an AI. Everything else works without one.

**Starting yard**

A new yard starts on a clear spring day. To let the season, the time or the weather change on its own, choose **Auto** for it. Whatever you choose is saved and stays as you left it.

**Sounds**

The **Yard sounds** switch and **Volume** are in **Place & atmosphere → Atmosphere**. Sounds start after your first interaction and play only while the yard is visible. Rain, birds, water and the other ambient sounds follow the place, the weather, the season and the time.

Friends chatting on their own is a separate setting, in **Friends & settings → Settings → Friends & AI**.

## Time of day and weather <Since v="0.1.0" />

Season and weather are set separately. Choose Snow in summer and snow falls over the summer landscape and outfits. Choose Clear in winter and the winter landscape stays. The indoor window and imported backgrounds work the same way. Auto weather still keeps the snow it picks to winter.

<Shot name="yard-spring" caption="A clear spring day in the yard. Auto time follows your clock when selected." />

1. Open **Place & atmosphere** at the bottom right.
2. Choose a background by its thumbnail under **Places**. Built-in and imported backgrounds share one gallery.
3. Choose the season, time and weather under **Atmosphere**.

- Changes apply right away, all on one page.
- **Import background** and **Background studio** sit beside the Places heading.
- Remove appears there only when an imported background is selected.

| Choice | Options |
|---|---|
| Place | Hill garden, Stone courtyard, Lakeside cottage, Cozy living room |
| Time of day | Auto time, Morning, Midday, Evening, Night |
| Weather | Auto weather, Clear, Rain, Snow |

**Places**

The three outdoor places each have their own house, four seasonal landscapes and their own fishing positions.

**Cozy living room** brings the same calm style indoors. Its lighting changes through morning, midday, evening and night, and the windows show spring blossoms, autumn leaves and winter snow. Rain and snow stay beyond the glass.

You can pet friends, chat, grow flowers and play the three games in every place. Fishing, wild bugs and the outdoor seasonal effects are only in the three gardens. Your place is remembered separately from your time and weather choices.

<Shot name="studio-night" caption="Cozy living room at night: a quiet village view beyond the glass and warm light indoors." />

With the time of day on **Auto**, the yard follows your computer's clock. The sky and ground change color through morning, midday, evening and night, and the sun crosses the sky from left to right. At night the moon shows tonight's phase.

With the weather on **Auto**, the yard picks new weather once every 20 minutes. It's mostly clear with rain now and then, and snow only in winter. It doesn't fetch the real weather from the internet.

The illustrated outdoor places are composed differently, from a sheltered courtyard to an open hillside and lake. Their colors change with the light. The background is repainted only when the scene or the window size changes.

With the season on **Auto**, the outdoor places follow the date:

| Season | Outdoor places show |
|---|---|
| Spring | Blossoms and falling petals |
| Summer | Deep green |
| Autumn | Red and gold leaves |
| Winter | Snowy ground |

You can also choose a season in **Place & atmosphere → Atmosphere**. If your computer's time zone is south of the equator, the automatic seasons are reversed.

When rain clears in daylight, a rainbow stands over the hills for a few minutes. On clear days a flock of birds crosses the sky now and then. Butterflies flutter until autumn, and fireflies glow on spring and summer nights.

If you pick a time of day or weather yourself, it stays fixed, and your choice is still there the next time you open the app.

When the weather changes, one or two friends say a word about it, like "Rain!" or "Snowman time?". Left alone, friends also talk to themselves in ways that fit the time of day.

## Let them chat <Since v="0.1.0" />

Press **Let them chat** in **Play → Group games** and the friends with an AI, up to three of them, have a short talk about the time of day and the weather. Their lines show one after another as speech bubbles in the yard.

With fewer than two friends connected to an AI, the button looks switched off. Press it or hover over it to see why. To join in yourself, use [group chat in the Friend panel](./chat.md).

To have them talk on their own now and then, turn on **Let them chat now and then** in **Friends & settings → Settings → Friends & AI**. They chat once every 20 minutes and rest while the window is hidden or someone is answering.

## The garden <Since v="0.1.0" />

Flower seeds and saplings both come from digging. Choose **Dig**, send a friend to open ground, and plant what they find. Neither turns up just because friends are strolling.

1. Click **Plant a seed** in **Play → Garden**. The number beside it is how many seeds you have.
2. Click a spot on the field. You can plant several in a row. Press **Esc** or the tool again to stop.

- Keyboard: press **Plant a seed** with Enter or Space, then press it again to plant beside the selected friend, as with the picnic.
- With a tool in hand, keys 1, 2 and 3 switch to **Dig**, **Plant a tree** and **Plant a seed**. They do nothing while you type in a field.

In Cozy living room, flowers sit in small planters instead of bare soil. Growth and your collection carry over when you change places.

Plants grow by the clock, even while the app is closed:

| After | Stage |
|---|---|
| 10 minutes | Sprout |
| 40 minutes | Leaves |
| 2 hours | Bud |
| 4 hours | Flower |

What flowers depends on the season you planted it in, with four kinds per season:

| Planted in | Flowers |
|---|---|
| Spring | Tulips, daffodils, crocuses, grape hyacinths |
| Summer | Sunflowers, hydrangeas, lavender, bellflowers |
| Autumn | Cosmos, chrysanthemums, dahlias, asters |
| Winter | Camellias, snowdrops, hellebores, cyclamen |

Hover over a plant to see what it is and how long until it flowers. Click it and the nearest friend walks over to water it. Friends also go and look at the flowers on their own.

To pull a plant up, right-click it or press **Delete** or **Backspace** on it. It goes at once, without a question. The notice at the bottom shows **Undo** for about six seconds, and Undo puts the plant back.

Only after that is the plant gone for good. If the app closes first, it stays. The seed doesn't come back.

- Limits: 20 seeds and 24 plants.
- Saved as `garden` in `state.json`.
- The **Album** records each kind of flower the first time it blooms, and every gift that comes.

### Gifts

Gifts come from friends, not from the clock. On a day you do something with a friend, such as patting it, giving it a treat, talking or fishing together, that friend may bring a gift. The whole yard gets at most one every other day, and time alone in the yard brings none. Once a gift may come, it takes 10 to 20 minutes, or five minutes for the first one.

Friends also bring a gift when they grow closer, one for each new level from **At ease** on. At **Very close** it's the friend's memento, marked with a small heart.

The wait is saved, and time with the app closed counts too, so you don't need to keep the app open. The friend jumps, tells you, and sets the gift down beside it.

The first gifts come in this order:

1. a bench
2. a lantern
3. a birdhouse
4. a little pond
5. a mailbox
6. a picnic blanket
7. a pinwheel
8. a campfire
9. a swing
10. a flowerpot
11. a stump stool
12. a tent
13. a signpost
14. a wind chime

After that, a friend brings one that isn't in the yard. The campfire glows from evening on, like the lantern. Friends sit on the swing and the stump, and the wind chime rings when a friend visits it.

Drag a decoration anywhere on the field to move it, or select it and use the arrow keys. Click it and the nearest friend walks over.

Friends also visit decorations on their own. They sit on the bench, gather on the blanket and look into the pond. The lantern is lit from evening on, the pond mirrors the sky, and a bird sits on the birdhouse by day.

To put one away, right-click it or press **Delete** or **Backspace** on it. It goes at once, and **Undo** in the notice brings it back for about six seconds. If the app closes first, it stays. A friend may bring it again another day.

- The yard holds 12 decorations. When it's full, a new gift waits in the **gift box** at the top of the album's Gifts book, which holds 12 more. Press one there to set it out once there's room.
- Putting one away keeps its album entry and makes room for the remaining gifts. A memento goes back into the gift box instead.
- The album has 20 gifts, including four delivered only during their calendar events. A memento is one of these 20, so it adds no new entry.

## Fishing and bugs <Since v="0.1.0" />

**Fishing**

Click visible water to fish by hand with the nearest free friend. When the float dips and **!** appears, click it. Too early or too late and the catch gets away.

**Play → Fish** has the selected friend finish the trip on its own. Both ways use that background's fishing spot. Your rod can also bring up freshwater prawns, crayfish and pond snails, and the album keeps them with the fish under **Aquatic life**.

**Bugs**

Bugs appear only in clear outdoor weather; rain and snow stop them. In the morning and at midday, click butterflies or dragonflies. On spring and summer evenings and nights, click fireflies. The nearest friend catches the bug.

**Fishing on its own**

From closeness level 3, an idle friend may go fishing on its own every 12 to 25 minutes while the yard is visible. Games, another fishing trip or an active placement tool pause it, and a catch isn't guaranteed.

**What you find**

What you find depends on the season, the time of day and the weather. Catfish and eels come out after dark, and more of them in the rain. Pond smelt appear only in winter, and a golden carp is very rare. Each fish is a different size every time.

A card shows what was caught and says so when it's new. A card for something the album keeps (a fish, a bug, a flower in bloom, a gift or a keepsake) has **See in album**, which opens the album at that book.

When something new arrives, **Album** in **Play → Find** and **Play** in the dock carry a small dot until you open the album.

**Album** (in **Play → Find**)

- Six books: Flowers, Aquatic life, Bugs, Gifts, Trees, Keepsakes.
- Every kind is listed:
  - caught: how many, and the biggest fish
  - not yet: a silhouette with when and where to look
- It shows when and where to look, and how long until a rare find can come again. It doesn't show exact percentages.
- Saved as `album` in `state.json`.

## Seasonal events <Since v="0.1.0" />

A few times a year the yard changes for a while. The event's name shows beside the scene label, a card tells you it has begun the first time you see it that year, and friends talk about it.

| Event | When | What it brings |
|---|---|---|
| Cherry blossom festival | April 1 to 20 | Petals drift everywhere and more butterflies come out; a friend brings a cherry sapling |
| Firefly nights | July 20 to August 20 | Many more fireflies at night, and a golden one to catch; a friend brings a watermelon |
| Harvest moon | September 8 to October 15 | On the nights around the event's first full moon (two days either side), as at Chuseok and the Mid-Autumn Festival, the moon rises big and golden; the other nights it keeps its phase. The moonlight carp comes to the lake at night; a friend brings a pumpkin |
| Winter lights | December 10 to 31 | Little lights along the trees, glowing from evening on; a friend brings a snowman |
| New Year | January 1 to 7 | Friends greet the new year |

An event's gift comes the first time a gift is due during the event, and only then. The album shows when each one comes.

South of the equator, judged by your computer's time zone, seasonal events come six months later. The new year stays in January.

## Closeness <Since v="0.1.0" />

Each friend grows closer to you through time in the yard and the things you do together. Hearts on its name tag show how close it is. The Friend panel shows its level, how far it is to the next one and what the next one brings.

Levels say how close you are, not what you are to each other. They read the same for a partner or a sibling as for a friend; that part is the [relationship](./chat.md#setting-a-relationship).

| Level | What it brings |
|---|---|
| Just met | |
| At ease | It welcomes you when the app opens and when you come back after a while |
| Warm | It goes fishing on its own now and then |
| Very close | It brings you its memento |
| Dearest | A flower on its name tag, and now and then it says something just for you |

Time together counts every ten minutes the yard is seen. Patting, treats, chatting, watering, catching and gifts count too, up to a day's worth. So a few minutes of clicking can't rush it: closeness comes over days, and nothing is ever lost by being away.

Closeness is saved as `bonds` in `state.json`.

## A little digging and tree planting

**Digging**

1. Open **Play** from the dock, or right-click a friend and choose **Play with** and its name.
   - **Ask Clover to dig** in the same menu (with that friend's name) picks up the dig tool, and that friend goes first.
2. **Dig** asks you to choose an open patch of ground. **Esc** cancels the choice.
3. The friend walks there and digs. No AI connection needed.

Water, furniture and roots aren't digging spots. If you take the friend away before it arrives, there's no discovery. To skip the panel, right-click bare ground and choose **Dig here**, and the friend digs that spot.

**The tool in hand**

The dig tool stays in your hand until you put it away. Each spot you choose sends the next free friend, and a friend that just dug rests a moment while another goes.

The strip that takes the dock's place at the bottom switches to **Plant a tree** or **Plant a seed** in one press. Its close button or **Esc** puts the tool away.

A tool in hand never hides the lake or the creatures. Press the water or a flying or crawling creature, and that same press puts the tool away and fishes or catches.

**Motions and outfits**

Daisy, Clover, Pip and Miso have their own motions in every season for digging, planting, casting, waiting for a bite, reeling in and reacting to a find. Imported pets join the same activities with their original animations.

Daisy, Pip and Miso wear the same everyday outfits in spring and autumn, and change into summer and winter outfits. Clover keeps the teal overalls and straw hat through autumn, then changes into a coat, scarf and knitted beanie for winter.

**What a dig finds**

- A flower seed, a sapling, one of seventeen keepsakes, or nothing. Keepsakes appear in **Album → Keepsakes**.
- After an uncommon find, all friends and places wait 24 hours for another uncommon find.
- Repeats happen, so completing the set can take longer.
- The same patch gives no further reward for 24 hours.
- Each friend waits eight seconds between digs.
- Limits: 20 seeds, and 20 saplings per species. A full pocket doesn't turn a reward into something else.

**Planting a tree**

1. Find a sapling by digging first.
2. Choose **Plant a tree** to see the stock: cherry, maple, pine, oak, birch, willow.
3. Pick a species you have and choose clear ground. Planting uses one matching sapling.

- Growth counts time with the app closed: young after two hours, mature after twelve.
- A mature tree is recorded once in **Album → Trees**.
- Each place holds six trees, up to twenty-four overall. Trees stay in the place where they were planted.
- Drag a tree to move it.
- To remove one at once, right-click it or press **Delete** on it. As with flowers, **Undo** in the notice brings it back for about six seconds. Its album entry stays, but no sapling comes back.
- Indoor places don't offer digging or tree planting. Imported backgrounds can explicitly allow or turn off soil.

## Games <Since v="0.1.0" />

The three games are in **Play → Group games**, and you play one at a time. While a game is on, its tile reads Stop with the game's name, such as **Stop Hide and seek**. Click it to end the game right away.

The other games, **Picnic**, **Plant a seed** and **Plant a tree** stay off for the whole game. When it ends, the friends walk back to where they were.

### Hide and seek

<Shot name="play-hide-and-seek" caption="Hide and seek. The line above the dock names the friend to find." />

1. Click **Hide and seek**. The friends scatter to their hiding places. A friend who doesn't reach a spot within 15 seconds hides where they are.
2. The line just above the dock says **Click to find** and the friend to find, then the other friends still hiding. Everything the yard tells you (a game, a find, a notice) appears in that same place.
3. Find the friend whose name is called and click them. If you're right, they jump out saying "You found me!" and the banner moves on to the next friend.
4. Click the wrong friend and you only get an "Oops, that's not them" notice. That friend keeps hiding, and the name to find stays the same.
5. When everyone is found, the game ends with "Everyone found!".

### Parade

1. Click **Parade**. The selected friend goes to the front and the others line up behind.
2. When the line is ready, they walk one lap around the yard.
3. After the lap, the friend in front says "Lap done!" and the game ends.

### Tag

**Tag** needs at least two friends.

1. The selected friend is it. They say "I'm it!" and "It" is added to their name tag.
2. The friend who is it chases the nearest friend while the others run away.
3. When it overlaps another friend on screen, that friend is caught. The caught friend becomes it, and the one who caught them is safe for a moment.
4. After a set time, the game ends with "Tag is over. Good running, everyone!".

## A window beside your work

Drag any edge or corner to resize the window, down to 300 × 210. To move it, drag the narrow transparent area along the top edge. Position and size are remembered, and at every size the same window keeps friends, chat, Invite, Settings, scene controls and games.

**Small windows** (640 pixels wide or less, or 480 pixels tall or less)

The dock hides in a small window. A round button appears in the bottom-right corner while the pointer is over the window, or when you reach it with Tab. It opens the same short menu as a right-click on empty scenery: **Place & atmosphere**, **Play**, **Friends & settings**, **Always on top**.

- **Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + P** opens Friends & settings.
- Each panel shows its actions as tiles whose names may take two lines. Section names are hidden.
- Longer panels scroll inside the window.

**Getting back**

- Closing Place & atmosphere or Play returns straight to the scenery.
- The Friend panel and Invite a friend have a Back action to Friends & settings. **×** returns to the yard.
- In Settings, Back goes from a section to the list of sections, then to Friends & settings.
- Invite a friend keeps Import visible in its header.

**Friend menu**

Click a friend's name tag to talk to it. Its tooltip says **Talk to** and the friend's name.

Right-click a friend for a short menu with **Talk to**, **Play with** and **Ask … to dig** for that friend (such as **Ask Clover to dig**). Play opens the same Play panel for it. Ask to dig picks up the dig tool, with that friend going first.

Close, Escape or a click outside takes you back to the scenery. There's no separate small-window play strip.

**Scenery menu**

Right-click empty scenery for the short action menu beside the pointer; in small windows, the corner button opens it too. It stays inside the window and scrolls when needed, and **Esc** or a click outside closes it. Friends, plants and decorations keep their own right-click actions.

**Fullscreen**

**Friends & settings → Window & desktop → Enter fullscreen** fills the screen. While you're in fullscreen, the same action reads **Return to previous size**, and **Esc** also takes you back. If a panel or menu is open, Esc closes it first.

Returning restores the previous window size, position and Always on top setting.

<Shot name="mini" caption="A small yard window beside your work." />

**Always on top**

Turn it on in **Friends & settings → Window & desktop → Always on top**, or with the checked action in the short right-click menu. It's also in the tray and the macOS View menu, and **Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + M** toggles it without resizing. It works independently of window size and is remembered.

On Linux, pinning depends on the desktop and window manager. Some Wayland desktops refuse the request; if the setting can't be applied, check your desktop's window rules.

<Shot name="mini-chat" caption="A conversation stays inside the small yard window, beside your work." />

**Close and quit**

**Friends & settings → Close window** hides the yard in the tray, and desktop friends and agent hooks keep running.

**Quit PlayPets** beside it closes PlayPets at once, the same as Quit in the tray and <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Q</kbd> on macOS. Conversations aren't saved. From the short right-click menu or the corner button, choose Friends & settings to reach these actions.

**Updates in small windows**

In a small window, an available update shows as a 32-pixel download icon with a dot. Its accessible label names the version, and activating it opens App updates directly. Larger windows keep the version pill.

**Dialogs and confirmations**

Friend settings, Import and AI connection dialogs keep their headings and actions in place while the form scrolls. The collection and confirmation dialogs scroll their own content.

A confirmation asks calmly, with **Cancel**, unless you'd lose something that can't come back. Deleting an AI connection, sending a friend away and deleting a background ask in red, and the other button reads **Keep it**.

Every confirmation also has a **Close** button. Flowers, trees and gifts aren't asked about; they offer **Undo** instead.

## Collecting at your own pace

The album has 100 entries across aquatic life, bugs, flowers, gifts, trees and little finds. Look for pond creatures, ladybugs and field crickets. Dig up a glass marble, a wooden whale, a felt mushroom, an acorn teacup or a sleeping fox figurine. And make room for a bunny watering can or a hedgehog planter from a friend.

**How long it takes**

The full 100-item album isn't a one-day or one-week goal, and there's no guaranteed completion date. The 16 ordinary gifts arrive on different local dates.

The four event gifts need festivals spread across the calendar, so depending on when you start, collecting them all can take months. Changing the season doesn't start a festival.

Flowers bloom four hours after planting and trees mature after twelve hours. Both count time with the app closed.

**Tips**

- **Fish:** rain makes catfish and eels easier to find. Choose a season and time when they appear, and use the guide to see what you might catch.
- **Bugs:** look in clear outdoor weather.
  - Morning and daytime: butterflies and dragonflies.
  - Spring and summer evenings and nights: fireflies.
  - Festivals follow the calendar; changing the season alone doesn't start one.
- **Digging:** season, weather and outdoor place change which sapling or keepsake is more likely.
  - Matching conditions favor some finds, but the others stay reachable.
  - Choose season and weather separately in Atmosphere, unusual combinations included.
  - Custom outdoor places still reflect season and weather.
  - The same patch keeps its result for 24 hours.
  - An unlucky streak doesn't make the next find more likely.
- **Space:** clearing a received gift keeps its album record and makes room for later gifts. A full seed or sapling pocket doesn't reroll the chosen reward.

The guide shows what you might discover under the chosen conditions. Water and obstacles leave fewer usable digging patches than the background's full grid.
