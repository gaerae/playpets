# The yard and games

The yard follows the clock, the weather and the seasons, and grows a little each day. Friends dig, fish, bring gifts and play with you, and what you find fills the album.

::: tip At a glance
| To | Do this | More |
|---|---|---|
| Change the place, season, time or weather | **Place & atmosphere** in the dock | [Time of day and weather](#weather) |
| Find seeds, saplings and keepsakes | **Play → Dig**, then click open ground | [Digging](#digging) |
| Grow flowers and trees | **Play → Plant a seed** or **Plant a tree** | [The garden](#the-garden) |
| Get gifts | Do something with a friend that day | [Gifts](#gifts) |
| Fill the album | Fish, catch bugs, dig, grow flowers and trees | [Album](#album) |
| See a seasonal event any day | Pick its season by hand, with the time and weather it asks for | [Seasonal events](#seasonal-events) |
| Grow closer to a friend | Pat, treat, chat and play a little every day | [Closeness](#closeness) |
| Play a game | **Play → Group games** | [Games](#games) |
| Keep the yard beside your work | Drag an edge or a corner | [A window beside your work](#a-window-beside-your-work) |
:::

## The dock

The scenery fills the window, and the dock at the bottom opens one panel at a time. Each panel is a single page split into sections, with no tabs or Back buttons inside.

| Dock | Holds |
|---|---|
| **Place & atmosphere** | **Places** (built-in and imported backgrounds in one gallery, **Import background**, **Background studio**), **Atmosphere** (season, time, weather, **Yard sounds**, **Volume**) |
| **Play** | Titled for the selected friend, such as **Play with Clover**. **Find**: **Dig**, **Fish**, **Album**. **Garden**: **Plant a seed** (with your seed count), **Plant a tree**, **Picnic**. **Group games**: the four games (**Snowman** only while the yard shows winter), **Flower viewing** while the cherry blossom festival is on, **Let them chat** |
| **Friends & settings** | **Friends & chat**, **Invite a friend**, **Window & desktop**, **Settings**, **Close window**, **Quit PlayPets** |

- **Let them chat** needs two friends connected to an AI. Everything else works without one.
- A new yard starts on a clear spring day. Choose **Auto** for the season, the time or the weather to let it change on its own. Every choice is saved.

### Sounds

- **Yard sounds** and **Volume**: **Place & atmosphere → Atmosphere**.
- Sounds start after your first interaction and play only while the yard is visible.
- Rain, birds, water and the other sounds follow the place, the weather, the season and the time.
- Friends chatting on their own is a separate setting: **Friends & settings → Settings → Friends & AI**.

## Time of day and weather <Since v="0.1.0" /> {#weather}

Pick each one yourself, or leave it on **Auto**. Changes apply at once.

<Shot name="yard-spring" caption="A clear spring day in the yard. Auto time follows your clock when selected." />

1. Open **Place & atmosphere** at the bottom right.
2. Under **Places**, choose a background by its thumbnail. **Remove** shows there only for an imported background.
3. Under **Atmosphere**, choose the season, the time and the weather.

| Choice | Options | On Auto |
|---|---|---|
| Place | Hill garden, Garden workshop, Wild garden, Lakeside cottage, your own backgrounds | |
| Season | Auto, Spring, Summer, Autumn, Winter | Outdoor places follow the date: blossoms and falling petals, deep green, red and gold leaves, snowy ground. South of the equator (by your computer's time zone), the seasons are reversed |
| Time of day | Auto time, Morning, Midday, Evening, Night | Follows your computer's clock, without showing a clock. The sky and ground change color, the sun crosses from left to right, and at night the moon shows tonight's phase |
| Weather | Auto weather, Clear, Rain, Snow | New weather every 20 minutes: mostly clear, rain now and then, snow only in winter. It isn't the real weather from the internet |

- A season, time or weather you pick stays fixed, and is still there the next time you open the app.
- Season and weather are separate. Snow in summer falls over the summer landscape and outfits; Clear in winter keeps the winter landscape. The indoor window and imported backgrounds work the same way.
- Picking a season by hand can also call up a [seasonal event](#seasonal-events) on any day.
- When the weather changes, one or two friends say a word about it, like "Rain!" or "Snowman time?". Left alone, friends talk to themselves in ways that fit the time of day.

### Weather moments

| Moment | When | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Rainbow | Rain clears in daylight; it stands over the hills for a few minutes | Dig where its end glimmers before it fades: you never come up empty-handed |
| Sunshower | Rain from a clear daytime sky | Watch the rain fall with the sun still out |
| Mist | Clear autumn mornings | Watch it lie on the field |
| Moonlit snow | Snow stops at night | Watch the snow glitter under the moon |
| Shooting stars | Clear summer nights | Look up: they come often, all night long |
| Birds | Clear days, now and then | Watch a flock cross the sky |

Bugs come with the light and the season too: see [Bugs](#bugs).

### Places {#places}

| Place | What it is |
|---|---|
| Hill garden | An open hillside, with its own house |
| Wild garden | Wildflowers around a clear little pool, left to nature |
| Lakeside cottage | A cottage by the lake, with its own house |
| Garden workshop | A glass-roofed workshop; the same look all year |

- Hill garden, Wild garden and Lakeside cottage each have four seasonal landscapes and their own fishing spots. Their colors change with the light.
- The background is repainted only when the scene or the window size changes.
- Your place is remembered apart from your time and weather choices.
- Growth and your collection carry over when you change places.

### Garden workshop <Since v="1.7.0" /> {#garden-workshop}

Two long potting tables, a tea corner with a checkers board, and a garden bench with a call bell at the front, all under a glass roof. The sky, the time of day and the weather show through the glass; rain and snow stay beyond it.

- Friends stroll, sort seeds at a table, have tea together or rest by the bench.
- A friend linked to a coding agent works at the tables and waits by the bell when the agent needs your approval ([what friends do there](./agents.md#where-friends-go)).

You can pet friends, chat, grow flowers, decorate the Christmas tree and play the games in every place. The rest differs:

| Activity | Hill garden, Wild garden, Lakeside cottage | Garden workshop |
|---|---|---|
| Flowers | In the ground | In small pots |
| Digging, fishing | Yes | No |
| Trees | Yes, six per place | No |
| Bugs | In clear weather | No |
| Seasonal landscapes and effects | Follow the season | No |
| Snowman, footprints in the snow | In winter | No |
| Spring shoots | In spring | No |
| Christmas night | On a snowy winter evening or night | No, the snow stays beyond the glass |

## Let them chat <Since v="0.1.0" />

Press **Play → Group games → Let them chat**, and up to three friends with an AI have a short talk about the time of day and the weather, as speech bubbles in the yard.

- Fewer than two friends with an AI: the button looks switched off. Press it or point at it to see why.
- To join in yourself, use [group chat in the Friend panel](./chat.md).
- To have them chat on their own, turn on **Let them chat now and then** in **Friends & settings → Settings → Friends & AI**. They talk once every 20 minutes, and rest while the window is hidden or someone is answering.

## The garden <Since v="0.1.0" />

Flower seeds and saplings both come from [digging](#digging), never from friends just strolling.

1. Choose **Play → Garden → Plant a seed**. The number beside it is how many seeds you have.
2. Click a spot on the field. Keep clicking to plant several; press **Esc** or the tool again to stop.

- Keyboard: press **Plant a seed** with Enter or Space, then again to plant beside the selected friend, as with the picnic.
- With a tool in hand, keys 1, 2 and 3 switch to **Dig**, **Plant a tree** and **Plant a seed**. They do nothing while you type in a field.

Plants grow by the clock, even while the app is closed:

| After | 10 minutes | 40 minutes | 2 hours | 4 hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stage | Sprout | Leaves | Bud | Flower |

What blooms depends on the season you planted in:

| Planted in | Flowers |
|---|---|
| Spring | Tulips, daffodils, crocuses, grape hyacinths |
| Summer | Sunflowers, hydrangeas, lavender, bellflowers |
| Autumn | Cosmos, chrysanthemums, dahlias, asters |
| Winter | Camellias, snowdrops, hellebores, cyclamen |

| On a plant | What happens |
|---|---|
| Point at it | Shows what it is and how long until it flowers |
| Click | The nearest friend walks over to water it. Friends also visit flowers on their own |
| Drag, or arrow keys | Moves it; it keeps growing there |
| Right-click → **Pull up**, or **Delete** or **Backspace** | Gone at once, no question. **Undo** in the notice brings it back for about six seconds; after that it's gone for good. If the app closes first, it stays. The seed doesn't come back |
| Right-click → **Show on desktop** | It grows on your desk too ([Desktop mode](./desktop.md#flowers-and-trees-on-the-desktop)) |

- Limits: 20 seeds and 24 plants.
- The **Album** records each kind of flower the first time it blooms.
- Saved as `garden` in `state.json`.

### Gifts {#gifts}

Gifts come from friends you spend time with, not from the clock.

| Gift | When it comes |
|---|---|
| Everyday gift | On a day you do something with a friend: pat it, give it a treat, talk or fish together. 10 to 20 minutes later (five for the very first). The whole yard gets at most one every other day; time alone in the yard brings none |
| Closeness gift | One for each new level from **At ease** on, on a day you play with that friend |
| Memento | At **Very close**, the friend's own gift, marked with a small heart for a day; after that the heart shows when you point at it or select it |
| Event gift | Only on a seasonal event's dates ([Seasonal events](#seasonal-events)) |

- The wait is saved and time with the app closed counts, so the app needn't stay open. The friend jumps, tells you and sets the gift down beside it.
- The first gifts come in this order: bench, lantern, birdhouse, little pond, mailbox, picnic blanket, pinwheel, campfire, swing, flowerpot, stump stool, tent, signpost, wind chime. After that, a friend brings one that isn't in the yard.

Friends use what they get. They sit on the bench, the swing and the stump, gather on the blanket and look into the pond, which mirrors the sky. The lantern and the campfire glow from evening on, a bird sits on the birdhouse by day, and the wind chime rings when a friend visits it.

| On a gift | What happens |
|---|---|
| Drag, or select it and use the arrow keys | Moves it anywhere on the field |
| Click | The nearest friend walks over |
| Right-click → **Put away**, or **Delete** or **Backspace** | Gone at once. **Undo** in the notice brings it back for about six seconds. If the app closes first, it stays. A friend may bring it again another day |
| Right-click → **Show on desktop** | Sends it out to the desktop ([Gifts on the desktop](./desktop.md#gifts-on-the-desktop)) |

- The yard holds 12 decorations. When it's full, a new one waits in the **gift box** at the top of the album's Gifts book, which holds 12 more. Press one there to set it out once there's room.
- Putting one away keeps its album entry and makes room for the gifts still to come. A memento goes back into the gift box instead, unless the box is full.
- Any gift that came before can come back out: **Set out** on its card in the album's Gifts book, once there's room. The snowman is the exception: you build it again.
- The album has 29 gifts, seven of them only on their events' dates. The snowman is one of the 29, but you build it ([Snowman](#snowman)). A memento is one of the 29 too, so it adds no new entry.

## Fishing and bugs <Since v="0.1.0" />

### Fishing

| Way | How |
|---|---|
| By hand | Click visible water; the nearest free friend casts. When the float dips and **!** appears, click it. Too early or too late and the catch gets away |
| **Play → Fish** | The selected friend goes and finishes the trip on its own |

- Both use that background's fishing spot.
- The rod also brings up freshwater prawns, crayfish, pond snails, mitten crabs, mussels and, now and then, a softshell turtle. The album keeps them with the fish under **Aquatic life**.
- What bites depends on the season, the time and the weather. Catfish and eels come out after dark, more of them in the rain. Pond smelt come only in winter, and a golden carp is very rare. Each fish is a different size every time.

### Bugs

Only outdoors in clear weather; rain and snow stop them. Click one and the nearest friend catches it.

| When | Bugs |
|---|---|
| Mornings and middays, spring to autumn | Butterflies and dragonflies; beetles and grasshoppers in the grass |
| Spring and summer evenings and nights | Fireflies |
| Autumn evenings and nights | Crickets in the grass |

### Fishing on its own

- From closeness level 3, an idle friend may go fishing on its own every 12 to 25 minutes while the yard is visible.
- Games, another fishing trip or a placement tool in hand pause it.
- When a fish bites, the friend calls you: press the float within a few seconds to reel it in together. Otherwise it gets away, so the album only keeps what you catch.

### Album

- **Play → Find → Album**. Eight books: Flowers, Aquatic life, Bugs, Gifts, Trees, Keepsakes, Footprints, Spring shoots.
- Every kind is listed. Caught: how many, and the biggest fish. Not yet: a silhouette with when and where to look.
- It shows how long until a rare find can come again, but no exact percentages.
- **Rare** marks the kinds that seldom come and everything that comes only with a seasonal event. An event's card gives its dates and the scene that calls it up.
- A catch shows a card, and says so when it's new. A card for something the album keeps (a fish, a bug, a flower in bloom, a gift, a keepsake or a spring shoot) has **See in album**, which opens that book.
- Something new puts a small dot on **Album** and on **Play** in the dock until you open the album.
- Saved as `album` in `state.json`.

## Seasonal events <Since v="0.1.0" /> {#seasonal-events}

A few times a year the yard changes for a while. Each event comes on its own on its dates, or on any day you pick its scene by hand in **Place & atmosphere → Atmosphere**.

| Event | On its own | Any day, pick |
|---|---|---|
| Cherry blossom festival | April 1 to 20, while the yard shows spring | <SceneChips lang="en" event="blossom" label="Any day, pick" /> |
| Firefly nights | July 20 to August 20, while the yard shows summer | <SceneChips lang="en" event="fireflies" label="Any day, pick" /> |
| Harvest moon | September 8 to October 15, while the yard shows autumn | <SceneChips lang="en" event="harvest" label="Any day, pick" /> |
| Winter lights | December 1 to 31, in any season | <SceneChips lang="en" event="lights" label="Any day, pick" /> |
| New Year | January 1 to 7, in any season | <SceneChips lang="en" event="newyear" label="Any day, pick" /> |

| Event | In the yard | Gift, on its dates only |
|---|---|---|
| Cherry blossom festival | Petals drift everywhere and more butterflies come out; friends gather for [flower viewing](#flower-viewing) | A cherry sapling |
| Firefly nights | More fireflies at night, and a golden one to catch | A watermelon |
| Harvest moon | The moonlight carp comes to the lake at night. Called up by hand, the full moon shows. On its own, the moon rises big and golden on the nights around the event's first full moon (two days either side, as at Chuseok and the Mid-Autumn Festival) and keeps its phase on the others | A pumpkin |
| Winter lights | Little lights along the trees, glowing from evening on | A small Christmas tree, then a baby reindeer, then Santa |
| New Year | Friends greet the new year | A kite |

- The event's name shows beside the scene label, a card says it has begun the first time you see it that year, and friends talk about it.
- With the season on **Auto**, only the dates bring an event. The calendar's own event comes first: December's lights stay whatever scene you pick.
- Event fish and bugs, such as the golden firefly and the moonlight carp, come whenever the event is on, either way.
- An event's gifts come the first times a gift is due during its dates, one each time, in a fixed order, and only then. A gift you have stays for good; the album shows when each one comes.
- South of the equator (by your computer's time zone), the cherry blossom festival, firefly nights and harvest moon come six months later. The winter lights and New Year keep their dates.

### December

In December, if the yard isn't showing winter, PlayPets asks once whether to show it until January 7. Say yes, and your own season comes back on January 8.

- The Christmas tree, the baby reindeer and Santa show in the yard and on the desktop from December 1 to January 7, and whenever the yard shows winter.
- The rest of the time they wait out of sight, still counting toward the 12 decorations. Put one away and it goes back into the gift box.

### Christmas night {#christmas-night}

In Hill garden, Wild garden or Lakeside cottage, with the little Christmas tree out, the tree lights up and grows into a grand tree on any day with this scene:

<SceneChips lang="en" :when="['season:winter', 'period:evening|night', 'weather:snow', 'gift:xmas_tree']" label="What it takes" />

- The grand tree stays while the snow falls, a bulb glinting now and then. It shrinks back when the snow stops, and grows again when the snow comes back.
- With the baby reindeer and Santa out too, they look up at night, and a sleigh crosses the moon each time the tree grows.
- A tree out on the desktop grows along with the one in the yard.

### Decorating the Christmas tree

Press the Christmas tree to decorate it.

- Each day, hang up to three things you've found in the album: flowers, trees, gifts and keepsakes. Aquatic life and bugs stay in the water and the grass.
- Each becomes a small ornament tinted by kind; point at one to see what it is.
- To change the tree, take an ornament down from its card. The day's three stay used.
- Ornaments stay on the tree in the yard, the grand tree too, until the next December starts with a bare tree.

### Flower viewing

While the cherry blossom festival is on, friends gather under a cherry tree you grew, look up and talk for a minute or so, then wander off.

| How | Details |
|---|---|
| On their own | Once a day, on a clear day from morning to evening, and only with **Let them chat now and then** on |
| By hand | **Play → Group games → Flower viewing**, once an hour. Greyed out while friends are already viewing or a game is on |
| Where | Under a cherry tree you grew; with none, on a picnic blanket or in the middle of the yard |
| Ends early | When the yard moves to another place, or night or rain comes. It never breaks into a game, fishing or digging |

- Friends connected to an AI talk about the blossom in their own words.
- On clear festival days, from morning to evening, a petal now and then lands on a friend's head. It stays until that friend moves, or night or rain comes. During a viewing, petals fall thicker and land more often.
- Click a falling petal to catch it: it twirls into a little glint, and the first one each day brings a wish from a friend.
- With reduced motion, petals don't fall and can't be caught, but friends still gather and petals still rest on heads.

### The year-end letter

On the first day you open PlayPets from December 26 to 31, the friend you're closest to writes you a letter about your year together: what you found for the album, a tree you grew, how close you've become and how long you've known each other. It asks how your year went and what you'd like to do next year.

- With a mailbox in the yard, the letter waits there; otherwise the friend tells you. The **Letter** chip beside the scene label and the yard menu open it until January 7.
- Write back in up to 200 characters, or skip it.
- On the first day you open PlayPets from January 1 to 7, a friend brings your answer back and cheers you on, and next December's letter looks back on it.
- A friend with an AI connection writes in its own words, and that AI reads your answer when it writes next year's letter.
- Letters and answers are kept on this computer; the letter card can delete them.

## Closeness <Since v="0.1.0" /> {#closeness}

Hearts on a friend's name tag show how close it is to you. The Friend panel shows its level, how far it is to the next one and what that brings.

| Level | What it brings |
|---|---|
| Just met | Where every friend starts |
| At ease | It welcomes you when the app opens and when you come back after a while |
| Warm | It goes [fishing on its own](#fishing-on-its-own) now and then |
| Very close | It brings you its memento |
| Dearest | A flower on its name tag, and now and then it says something just for you |

- Levels say how close you are, not who you are to each other; that is the [relationship](./chat.md#setting-a-relationship).
- Time together counts every ten minutes the yard is seen, up to an hour's worth a day.
- Patting, treats, chatting, watering, catching and gifts count more, up to a day's worth. Pats alone fill only a quarter of that.
- So leaving the window open or a minute of clicking can't rush it, and nothing is lost by being away.
- Saved as `bonds` in `state.json`.

## Digging and trees

### Digging

1. Choose **Play → Find → Dig**, or right-click a friend and choose **Ask Clover to dig** (with that friend's name) so it goes first.
2. Click an open patch of ground. **Esc** cancels.
3. The friend walks there and digs. No AI needed.

- Shortcut: right-click bare ground and choose **Dig here**.
- Water, furniture and roots aren't digging spots. Take the friend away before it arrives and there's no find.
- The tool stays in your hand: each spot you choose sends the next free friend, and a friend that just dug rests a moment.
- The strip that replaces the dock switches to **Plant a tree** or **Plant a seed** in one press. Its close button or **Esc** puts the tool away.
- A tool in hand never hides the lake or the creatures: pressing water or a creature puts the tool away and fishes or catches.

| A dig finds | Rules |
|---|---|
| A flower seed, a sapling, one of 25 keepsakes (**Album → Keepsakes**), or nothing | After a rare find, every friend and place waits 24 hours for another. The same patch gives nothing more for 24 hours. Each friend waits eight seconds between digs. Repeats happen, so a full set can take a while |

- Limits: 20 seeds, and 20 saplings per kind. A full pocket doesn't turn a find into something else.

### Planting a tree

1. Find a sapling by digging.
2. Choose **Play → Garden → Plant a tree** to see your stock: cherry, maple, pine, oak, birch, willow, ginkgo, persimmon, bamboo, magnolia, crape myrtle and dawn redwood.
3. Pick a kind you have and click clear ground. It uses one sapling.

- Growth counts time with the app closed: young after two hours, mature after twelve. A mature tree is recorded once in **Album → Trees**.
- Six trees per place, 24 in all. A tree stays in the place where it was planted.
- Drag a tree to move it. Right-click → **Remove**, or **Delete**: gone at once, with **Undo** for about six seconds. Its album entry stays; the sapling doesn't come back.
- Imported backgrounds can allow or turn off soil.

### Footprints in the snow

In a winter yard, faint animal footprints cross the snow: one trail a day, plus another on a morning after a snowy night (only one a day from December 26 to January 7).

- Click the prints (or Tab to them and press Enter), and a rested friend follows them, sniffing.
- At the end, a tail peeks out from behind a bush and is gone. Something small waits there to dig up, and each animal has finds it likes.
- An hour of new snow covers the prints; the next day they're gone.
- **Album → Footprints** shows each print until you follow it, then the tail and the name.

### Spring shoots

While the yard shows spring outdoors, a few shoots come up after rain, and one on a fair morning. At most three stand at a time, each for a few hours.

- Click a shoot, and the selected friend, or the nearest free one, digs it up.
- **Album → Spring shoots** holds eight: shepherd's purse, mugwort, fiddlehead, wild garlic, bamboo shoot, horsetail shoot, asparagus and morel. It's for collecting, not a guide to wild plants.

### Motions and outfits

- Daisy, Clover, Pip and Miso have their own motions in every season for digging, planting, casting, waiting for a bite, reeling in and reacting to a find. Imported pets join in with their own animations.
- Daisy, Pip and Miso wear everyday outfits in spring and autumn, and change for summer and winter. Clover keeps the teal overalls and straw hat through autumn, then wears a coat, scarf and knitted beanie in winter.

## Games <Since v="0.1.0" />

The four games are in **Play → Group games**, one at a time. While one is on, its tile reads Stop with its name, such as **Stop Hide and seek**: click it to end the game. The other games, **Picnic**, **Plant a seed** and **Plant a tree** stay off until it ends, then friends walk back to where they were.

| Game | How it goes | Ends |
|---|---|---|
| Hide and seek | Friends hide (one that can't reach a spot in 15 seconds hides where it is). The line above the dock says **Click to find**, the friend to find, then the others still hiding. Click the right friend and it jumps out with "You found me!", and the line moves on. A wrong one only gets "Oops, that's not them" and keeps hiding | "Everyone found!" |
| Parade | The selected friend leads, the others line up behind, and once the line is ready they walk one lap of the yard | "Lap done!" |
| Tag | Needs two friends. The selected friend is it ("It" on its name tag) and chases the nearest; overlapping on screen catches a friend, which becomes it, while the catcher is safe for a moment | "Tag is over. Good running, everyone!" after a set time |
| Snowman | While the yard shows winter. Two friends roll a snowball, and three stack into a snowman ([Snowman](#snowman)) | When the snowman stands |

<Shot name="play-hide-and-seek" caption="Hide and seek. The line above the dock names the friend to find." />

Everything the yard tells you (a game, a find, a notice) shows in that same line above the dock.

### Snowman {#snowman}

1. While the yard shows winter, choose **Play → Group games → Snowman**. Now and then a friend suggests one and starts rolling on its own.
2. Two friends roll a snowball that grows as it goes. Press it to help push, or drag it (or use the arrow keys) to roll it yourself. A soft blue trail fades behind it.
3. Three balls stack into a snowman, with a few sparkles.
4. Choose its hat, scarf and carrot nose. To change them later, press the snowman or choose **Dress up** in its menu.

- One a day. Ask again and a friend says they built one already.
- A new snowman replaces the old one, which melts away; with company, a friend says goodbye to it.
- It stays in the yard only while the yard shows winter. **Show on desktop** in its menu takes it out, dressed the same, while the yard shows winter. Putting it away melts it.
- With 12 decorations in the yard and no snowman, a friend says the yard is full, and the game doesn't start.

## A window beside your work

Drag any edge or corner to resize the window, down to 300 × 210, and drag the narrow transparent strip along the top to move it. Position and size are remembered, and every size keeps friends, chat, Invite, Settings, scene controls and games. To use the whole desktop as a yard, turn on [Desktop mode](./desktop.md); it works with any window size.

<Shot name="mini" caption="A small yard window beside your work." />

### Small windows

A window is small at 640 pixels wide or less, or 480 tall or less.

- The dock hides. A round button shows in the bottom-right corner while the pointer is over the window, or when you Tab to it. It opens the same short menu as a right-click on empty scenery: **Place & atmosphere**, **Play**, **Friends & settings**, **Always on top**.
- **Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + P** opens Friends & settings.
- Panels show their actions as tiles (names may take two lines), hide section names and scroll inside the window.
- An available update shows as a 32-pixel download icon with a dot instead of the version pill. Its label names the version, and it opens App updates.

### Window controls

| Control | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Enter fullscreen** | **Friends & settings → Window & desktop** | Fills the screen. It then reads **Return to previous size**, and **Esc** also returns (closing an open panel or menu first). Size, position and Always on top come back |
| **Always on top** | **Window & desktop**, the short right-click menu, the tray, the macOS View menu, **Cmd/Ctrl + Shift + M** | Keeps the yard above other windows, at any size, remembered. On Linux it depends on the desktop: some Wayland desktops refuse it, so check your window rules |
| **Close window** | **Friends & settings** | Hides the yard in the tray; desktop friends and agent hooks keep running |
| **Quit PlayPets** | **Friends & settings**, the tray, <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Q</kbd> on macOS | Closes PlayPets at once. Conversations aren't saved |

### Dialogs and confirmations

- Friend settings, Import and AI connection dialogs keep their headings and actions in place while the form scrolls. The collection and confirmation dialogs scroll their own content.
- A confirmation asks calmly, with **Cancel**, unless you'd lose something that can't come back: deleting an AI connection, sending a friend away and deleting a background ask in red, with **Keep it** as the other button.
- Every confirmation also has a **Close** button. Flowers, trees and gifts aren't asked about; they offer **Undo** instead.

## Collecting at your own pace

The album has 169 entries across aquatic life, bugs, flowers, gifts, trees, footprints, spring shoots and little finds. Look for Korean aucha perch and mitten crabs, a rare sturgeon on cold nights, mantises in the grass, and katydids and crickets on autumn nights. Grow dandelions, morning glories, red spider lilies and pansies, plant a ginkgo or a persimmon tree, dig up a pinecone, a paper boat or, now and then, a four-leaf clover, and make room for a water wheel, a hammock or a crock stand from a friend.

### How long it takes

- The full album isn't a one-day or one-week goal, and there's no set finish date.
- The 21 ordinary gifts arrive on different days.
- The seven event gifts come only on their events' dates, spread across the calendar, so depending on when you start, collecting them all can take months. Picking a season calls up an event's scene, not its gifts.
- Flowers bloom four hours after planting and trees mature after twelve, counting time with the app closed.

### Tips

| For | Tip |
|---|---|
| Fish | Rain makes catfish and eels easier to find. Pick a season and time when they appear, and check the discovery guide for what might bite |
| Event fish and bugs | Pick the event's season with the time and weather it asks for, and the golden firefly or the moonlight carp can come on any day |
| Digging | Season, weather and outdoor place make some saplings and keepsakes more likely, but the others stay reachable. Choose season and weather separately in Atmosphere, unusual pairs included; your own outdoor backgrounds reflect them too |
| The same patch | It keeps its result for 24 hours. An unlucky streak doesn't make the next find more likely |
| Space | Putting a gift away keeps its album entry and makes room for later gifts. A full seed or sapling pocket doesn't reroll a find |

The discovery guide shows what you might find under the conditions you choose. Water and obstacles leave fewer digging patches than the background's full grid.
