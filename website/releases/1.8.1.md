# 1.8.1

<ReleaseMeta />

Grown fruit trees drop their fruit in its season, and a friend picks it up when you click it. In winter friends walk over to a red plum to smell its blossom.

<Downloads version="1.8.1" />

## What changed

- Chestnuts, persimmons and oaks drop fruit in autumn, figs and red plums in summer, yuzu in winter.
- Click a fallen fruit and a friend picks it up, with a special line for the day's first one.
- Fallen fruit stays where it fell for a while, also when you visit another place and come back.
- In winter a friend sometimes stands under a red plum in bloom and smells the blossom.
- The red plum is drawn fuller, with crimson blossom along every branch.
- Friends out on the desktop use less CPU while they stand about.

How trees grow is in [The yard and games](../guide/play.md).
