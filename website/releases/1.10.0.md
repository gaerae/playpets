# 1.10.0

<ReleaseMeta />

The yard now looks like a game, in cream paper and honey wood, and friends walk with real steps, all the way out onto a strip of grass along your desktop.

<Downloads version="1.10.0" />

## What changed

- The yard's panels, the album and every dialog wear cream paper and honey-wood frames.
- **Play** is the honey-gold button in the dock, and the album opens as a book with ribbon tabs.
- Daisy, Clover, Pip and Miso walk with real steps: toward you, away from you, and at a run.
- A friend's feet stay where they step, and it turns in an arc and eases into its stop.
- Click open grass and the selected friend walks there, to a ring of bent grass.
- <Only os="Windows" /> **Update with Microsoft Store** updates the app from inside it, with progress.
- Friends stop to look at each other, meet face to face, sit for a rest and stretch.
- In the Wild garden, friends walk far back across the meadow and look smaller there.
- Friends are a little larger, and a name tag shows when you point at a friend or it talks.
- Daisy looks about 11 or 12 now, with longer hair and new clothes in cream and butter yellow.
- Any pet can bring its own walks, runs and poses for digging, planting and fishing.
- Friends step out onto a strip of grass along the desktop's bottom; drag a pebble to resize it.
- **Grass on desktop**, beside **Stroll on desktop**, turns it on and off; in winter it snows over.
- Desktop friends rest longer between gestures, and moving friends use less CPU.
- Ten winter decorations come after Santa, from ribbon trees to a gingerbread house.
- The new decorations light up from evening, and the wreath can hang from a screen's top.
- The little Christmas tree is redrawn in gold and cream, and glows warmly on Christmas night.
- On Christmas night the star opens soft rays, and the sleigh leaves a long ribbon of light.
- Each album card shows how its kind is found in pictures: **How**, **When** and **Best in**.
- A card takes back what it set out: **Put back**, or − and + when you found more.
- Cards wear **New**, **Rare** or **Best ever**, and a green **Now** marks what the yard gives now.
- An animal you follow three times turns to look, then comes out to the yard and the desktop.
- A full book shows a card of its own, and the album has 213 entries, every grown tree counted.
- The yard holds 12 of every kind, and gifts that come while it is full wait in the album.
- Light glints on the water, cloud shadows cross the field and grass sways in the corners.
- **Yard sounds** rest between calls: birds, cicadas, geese, frogs and crickets, each in season.
- A painted hand shows what to try next, from patting a friend to opening the album.
- A finished game brings every friend in it closer, and in **Tag** a click holds whoever is it.
- **Place & atmosphere** shows seasons, times and weather as pictures, with **Now** on **Auto**.
- Settings end every row with its switch and fold away the coding tools you don't use.
- **Play** and **Place & atmosphere** have their own keys and open from the tray menu too.
- In small and short windows, panels and Settings keep their titles clear and fit without scrolling.
- A click beside a friend reaches what is behind it, on the desktop the window behind.
- Hearts, sparkles and menu checks are drawn, so they look the same on every system.
- Treetops and hills meet the sky smoothly, with no pale outline or white patches.
