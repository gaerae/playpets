# 1.8.7

<ReleaseMeta />

Friends stand calmly now, turn to look at you, and walk, lift and land with a little weight.

<Downloads version="1.8.7" />

## What changed

- A standing friend mostly stands and does its little gesture now and then, at its own moment.
- A friend looking at the pointer turns toward it bit by bit instead of jumping.
- Walks set off slowly and ease into their stop, and the friend leans with the pace.
- A friend you carry lifts off the ground and swings as you pull, then drops and lands.
- Friends in the yard have a soft shade under their feet.
