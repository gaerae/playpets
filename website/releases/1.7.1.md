# 1.7.1

<ReleaseMeta />

Friends walk smoothly instead of in small hops, and what you set out in the yard stands on the ground: smaller farther back, with a shadow at its feet and the light of the hour.

<Downloads version="1.7.1" />

## What changed

- Friends walk smoothly in the yard and on the desktop, without small hops or a jump as they set off.
- Butterflies, dragonflies, fireflies and falling petals move smoothly, with no flicker.
- Gifts, flowers and trees farther back look smaller, as friends do.
- Gifts, flowers and trees stand on their feet; a bench or a swing has a shadow under each leg.
- Gifts, flowers and trees take the light of the morning, the evening and the rain.
- In the Garden workshop, gifts match the furniture's size and can't go inside it.
- On the desktop, gifts, flowers and trees come down to the bottom of the screen when you let go.
- At the back of the Hill garden, trees and flowers stand on the lawn, not in the hedge or the bushes.
- When a place's picture can't be loaded, everything moves onto the painted land at once.

Planting and setting out gifts are in [The yard and games](../guide/play.md).
