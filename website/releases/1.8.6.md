# 1.8.6

<ReleaseMeta />

Friends take their time now: they walk at an easy pace in their own steps, in a small window and on a full screen alike.

<Downloads version="1.8.6" />

## What changed

- Friends walk at an easy pace and stroll a few steps at a time, the same in any window.
- Games keep that pace too: no racing in hide-and-seek, and room between friends in a parade.
- Name tags show only the name; how close a friend is shows in the Friend panel.
- The album opens on its books, and each hint says what finding that kind takes.
- Resizing the window keeps the bugs, leaves, petals, rain and stars that are out.
- Resizing the window mid-dig still tells the find, and a picnic's cloth moves with the yard.

How close you are is explained in [Closeness](../guide/play.md#closeness), and the hints in [Album](../guide/play.md#album).
