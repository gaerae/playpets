# 1.4.0

<ReleaseMeta />

Gifts now come from friends, not from the clock: play with a friend and it may bring you something, and growing closer brings one too.

<Downloads version="1.4.0" />

## What changed

- A gift comes at most every other day, from a friend you did something with that day.
- A friend brings a gift each time it grows closer, and at Very close a memento of its own.
- A gift box in the album keeps gifts that came while the yard was full.
- Closeness grows from play: time with the yard open counts for up to an hour a day.
- A friend fishing on its own calls you when a fish bites; press the float to reel it in.
- Import a pet from a ZIP file, and remove a pet you no longer want from Invite a friend.
- Imported backgrounds and the place, season and weather you picked stay with your data.
- A Claude Code friend waits for you only when Claude asks, and says when a turn fails.
- The chat log keeps up to 500 messages a session, and a friend stays itself in a long chat.
- <Only os="macOS, Linux" /> Claude Code and Codex CLI installed with nvm, fnm, Volta and others are found.
- <Only os="Windows" /> The Store version opens pet gallery links, and Start at login works.
- Fixes for saving, the tray, digging, hide and seek, trees, moving gifts and the Places buttons.
