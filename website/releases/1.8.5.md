# 1.8.5

<ReleaseMeta />

Friends talk like friends again: only their own words, and no guessing who you are.

<Downloads version="1.8.5" />

## What changed

- Friends say only their words, without a long action in brackets before what they say.
- Friends no longer guess whether you are a man or a woman when they call you.
- Footprints in the snow and the bush at their end shrink with a smaller window.

To choose what a friend calls you, see [Chat with a friend](../guide/chat.md).
