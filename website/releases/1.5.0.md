# 1.5.0

<ReleaseMeta />

Your desk can have a bench now: send friends and gifts out one at a time, and a strolling friend stops by to say hello. The album grows to 150 finds.

<Downloads version="1.5.0" />

## What changed

- Right-click a friend and choose **Show on desktop** to send just that friend out.
- Gifts go out to the desktop too, and strolling friends stop by them to say something.
- Press **Set out** in the album to bring back any gift that came before.
- A right-click on the desktop lets you talk to a friend or take it off the desktop.
- The album grows to 150: new fish, bugs, flowers, trees, keepsakes and six gifts.
- The album reads like a collector's notebook, with a star for each finished book.
- Where a rainbow ends the ground glimmers, and a dig there always finds something.
- Sunshowers, autumn mist, moonlit snow and summer meteor showers, each pointed out.
- Ask a friend about the app and it answers from the app's own facts.
- Fish and bugs turn up as often as they are common; event-only finds are marked rare.
- A memento's heart shows for a day, then only when you point at the memento.
- Downloads are smaller: the macOS disk image is about 200 MB instead of 315 MB.
- Switching books in the album starts at the top of the new book.
- <Only os="Windows" /> The Store version's icon no longer sits on a blue square.
- <Only os="Windows" /> In the Store version, **Open folder** opens File Explorer again.

## Also

- Each starter friend downloads by itself from the [front page](../index.md), ready for ChatGPT and Codex too.
- On Windows, the download section also offers the installer (.exe); see [Install](../guide/install.md).

Sending friends and gifts out is in [Desktop mode](../guide/desktop.md); the new finds are in [The yard and games](../guide/play.md).
