# 1.6.1

<ReleaseMeta />

The yard feels more alive: friends bob along as they walk, look smaller farther back, and trees and flowers sway in the breeze.

<Downloads version="1.6.1" />

## What changed

- Friends bob along as they walk and settle as they stop.
- Friends farther back look a little smaller, and strolls no longer end on a tree or a flower.
- Friends walk down to the foot of the yard, with their name tags still in view.
- Outdoors, trees and flowers sway in the breeze.
- A double-click on a friend is two pats. To talk, click its name tag.
- Small windows fit better: the tools sit in one row and a new find's card takes one line.
- Panels come in three sizes and open in their usual place, from the right-click menu too.
- Text is never smaller than 11px.
- A flower keeps its kind a surprise until its bud shows.
- Planting a tree lists only the saplings you have.
- A gift or a friend behind a tree or a flower can be clicked through its empty corners.
- The rainbow's end glimmers only on ground not dug that day.
- The album counts grown trees in their own words and says a gift was received.
- A snowman wears its scarf around its neck and its hat on top of its head.
- A snowman can go out on the desktop too, dressed as in the yard, and a tree keeps its ornaments.
- Christmas night comes on any snowy winter night, and the desktop tree grows too.
- Flowers and planted trees can go out on the desktop from their menu, and flowers can be moved.
- In dark mode, a few buttons and panels that stayed light now darken.
