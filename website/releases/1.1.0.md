# 1.1.0

<ReleaseMeta />

PlayPets 1.1.0 is in development. The download below becomes available after the GitHub release is published.

<Downloads version="1.1.0" />

## Changes in preparation

- Meet Daisy, Clover, Pip and Miso through bespoke digging, planting, fishing and discovery reactions in every seasonal outfit. Imported pets continue to use their original animation sheets.
- Fill a 100-entry album with seasonal flowers, freshwater life, insects, gifts, trees and small keepsakes. The guide gives habitat clues without reducing each discovery to a percentage.
- Find saplings by digging and keep them in your pocket. Planting uses one sapling from your stock; choose from cherry, maple, pine, oak, birch and willow. Trees keep growing while the app is closed.
- Collect seventeen keepsakes. Seeds come from digging, so flower seeds and tree saplings share the same exploration loop.
- Automatic update checks stay quiet: 20 seconds after startup, then every 6 hours, with quiet retries after failures. They reveal an available version without opening Settings or installing it.
- Settings → About brings the result, release notes, download progress, errors and retry together. Choose **Install and restart** when ready; closing Settings during download lets it continue without starting a second download when reopened.
- Microsoft Store packages retain their independent package-managed update path.
- Compact scenery, play and management controls keep the yard visible in both small and large windows.

Read the [discovery guide](../guide/play.md#discovery-guide) and [update instructions](../guide/install.md#updates) for details. Microsoft Store certification and publication remain separate from GitHub publication.
