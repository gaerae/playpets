# 0.1.0

<Downloads version="0.1.0" />

This is the first public release. Look after friends in the yard, send them out onto the desktop, chat through the Claude Code or Codex subscription you already use, and watch your friends react while a coding agent works.

### Added
- The yard: pat your friends and put down treats. Friends stroll through scenery that changes with the time of day, the weather and the season (the sun and moon follow the clock, the moon shows its phase, and blossoms, autumn leaves and a rainbow after rain come and go), and play hide and seek (find by name), parade and tag. Click a friend's name tag to chat. Patting shows hearts, and treats and an agent's finished work scatter sparkles. With reduced motion turned on, these effects all stop.
- A painted yard: hills, trees and clouds are painted like the background of an animated film, with round trees lit from the sun's side and a small lake in the valley that mirrors the sky.
- Fishing and bugs: click the lake and a friend goes fishing (click the float when it dips); click a butterfly, dragonfly or firefly and a friend leaps for it. Nineteen fish and sixteen bugs by season, time and weather (one of each only during an event) fill an album. A clock chip at the top right of the yard shows the time and date.
- Seasonal events: cherry blossoms, firefly nights, the harvest moon, winter lights and the New Year change the yard for a while, each with a gift that comes only then.
- Yard sounds: a breeze, the lake, birds by day and crickets on warm nights, with small notes for what friends do. Quiet by default and easy to turn off (the speaker by the clock, Settings, or Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+S).
- Closeness: friends grow closer through time in the yard and what you do together. Hearts on the name tag show how close; each level brings something new, from a welcome back to fishing on its own.
- The garden: friends find seeds on their strolls; plant them and they grow by the clock into one of the two flowers of their season. The yard becomes your own over the days. Once a day a friend also brings a gift for it, fourteen in all, from a bench and a lantern to a campfire, a swing and a wind chime, to drag wherever you like.
- Starter pets: Tabby, Smoky and Mochi come with the app, so you can invite a friend on the very first start. They are under **PlayPets starter pets** in the Invite tab.
- On first start the empty yard asks who to play with and shows the starter pets: one click brings one in. A small card then lists what is left and ticks off what is done. With Claude Code or Codex CLI on the computer, one **Connect now** gives every friend an AI with no key.
- Start at login: one switch in Settings. With desktop mode on, only your friends come out at login.
- Mini mode: shows only the yard in a small window that stays on top, in the screen's corner the first time and where you left it after that, with the care buttons rising under the pointer.
- Desktop mode: friends come out on every monitor and stroll around, can be dragged, and follow the mouse with their eyes. Point at a friend and its name shows as a button that opens the chat. They float on top of other apps' full screen too (macOS).
- Chat: connect an AI for each friend (Claude Code or Codex subscription, Ollama, OpenAI-compatible server). Set a personality or change it through the chat, and have several friends talk together.
- The Friend tab reads like a messenger: the friend's name, state, relationship chip and closeness row at the top, and a **⋯** menu for friend settings, the AI connection, clearing the chat and sending the friend away. A conversation not yet started offers a few first lines to pick, and each friend's turn opens with its picture.
- Relationships: in Friend settings, choose Friend, Partner, Dependable mentor or Playful younger sibling from cards that say the voice each brings, and fill in **What it calls you**. The way your friend talks and what it calls you change, and friends chatting together know each other's relationship. A partner, a mentor and a younger sibling speak in voices of their own even without an AI.
- Let them chat: press **Let them chat** in the yard toolbar and the friends with an AI talk among themselves about the time and the weather. Turn on **Let them chat now and then** in Settings and they do it every 20 minutes on their own.
- Coding agent hooks: through Claude Code, Gemini CLI and Cursor hooks, Codex notifications and MCP, friends react while the agent works and tell you about recent work. Each agent can report to a friend you choose, and the agent shows on the name tag and as a chip in the Friend tab. The dot by a friend's head shows the agent's state (working, waiting, done, trouble). The app executable receives it directly, without Node.js.
- Import a pet: from a community pet page address, a GitHub folder or a folder on your computer. Opening a `playpets://import?url=<pet page address>` link asks first, then imports that pet and opens the Invite tab. The Invite tab groups pets by where they are, and the **Type** filter (All, People, Animals, Other) narrows it down.
- Friends are never lost to a missing pet file: if a pet folder is moved or unplugged, its friend waits with its closeness kept, a notice says how many are waiting, and it comes back once the file does.
- Languages: 한국어, English, 日本語 and 简体中文. Follows the system language at first; change it in Settings. The desktop windows switch to the new language too.
- Updates and menus: a newer version shows as a button in the top bar and a dot on the **Settings** tab, and opens an update window with **What's new**, **Later** and **Install and restart** (with download progress). Settings is grouped into General, The yard, Coding agent hooks and **About PlayPets** (the version, updates and the project's pages). The tray menu shows mini mode, desktop mode and strolling with check marks, and its icon gets a small mark when a coding agent is waiting for you; the tray menu and the macOS menu bar have Settings, Check for updates and About; <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd> or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> <kbd>,</kbd> opens Settings. On macOS a **View** menu opens the Friend and Invite tabs and switches mini mode and desktop mode; the same shortcuts (Cmd/Ctrl+1, +2, +Shift+M, +Shift+D) work on every system.
- Installer screens: the macOS disk image shows the yard and asks you to drag the app to Applications, and the Windows installer carries pet artwork. Linux software centers show a description and screenshots.
- Release files: the GitHub release has a macOS dmg (Apple Silicon and Intel), the Windows installer and portable zip (x64, arm64), and Linux deb and AppImage (x64, arm64). Only the trial MSIX is a local build.

### Security
- Importing a pet fetches files only from the site of the pasted address (its subdomains, and the hosts of a listed community), and does not follow a page that points elsewhere. Private addresses are blocked when the name is resolved.
- A pet file's description reaches the AI marked as a description, not an instruction, and a personality saved during a chat keeps that mark.

### Known limitations
- The release files are not code-signed, so macOS Gatekeeper and Windows SmartScreen block the first launch. How to get past it is in [Install](../guide/install.md).
- The Windows build was checked in a virtual machine (the x64 installer on Windows 11 ARM), installing and running, but has not been tested enough on real machines yet.
