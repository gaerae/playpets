## What is included

- Three illustrated starter companions: Daisy, Clover and Pip, with summer and winter outfits. Import compatible community pets or create your own with the [character studio](../guide/creator-kit.md).
- Four places, from outdoor gardens to a cozy living room. Choose the season, time and weather in **Place & atmosphere**, or import a background made with the [background studio](../guide/background-kit.md).
- A yard that fills the window, with compact place and menu controls. Mini mode hides those controls; desktop mode lets friends stroll over other windows.
- Pat friends, share a picnic, grow flowers, fish, catch bugs and collect gifts. Play hide and seek, parade and tag. Seasonal events and optional sounds bring the scene to life.
- Optional AI conversations through a local Ollama model, a signed-in Claude Code or Codex CLI, or an OpenAI-compatible server. Provider usage limits and charges apply. Games and gardening need no AI connection.
- Coding-agent hooks and MCP let friends react to work. Korean, English, Japanese and Simplified Chinese are available in the app and user guide.
- An update button appears before the place selector when a newer version is available. The update dialog shows download, verification and installation progress; after restart, a brief message confirms the installed version.

## Before installing

The first public release and its signed update feed are still being prepared. The macOS app is not notarized, and trusted publisher signing is not yet configured for the installers. Security warnings may appear. Linux packages remain under dependency review while stable Tauri completes its GTK migration. Read the [installation guide](../guide/install.md) for platform requirements and the available options. The Windows portable ZIP requires manual replacement for updates.
