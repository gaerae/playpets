# 1.8.4

<ReleaseMeta />

Planted trees now stand in every outdoor place, flowers and trees go as low as gifts do, and those on the left stay in sight.

<Downloads version="1.8.4" />

## What changed

- Planted trees stand in every outdoor place, as flowers and gifts do. The yard holds up to six.
- Flowers and trees can be planted and moved as low as gifts go, just above the dock.
- A tree or a flower at either side of the yard shows whole.
- A tree, a flower or a gift with a friend behind it stays in sight, and the friend shows through.
- <Only os="macOS" /> A tree or a flower on the left no longer disappears in the morning, the evening, rain or at night.

How trees grow is in [The yard and games](../guide/play.md).
