# 1.7.0

<ReleaseMeta />

Friends who help a coding agent get a glass-roofed garden workshop. The outdoor places have new pictures in one calm tone, and the old courtyard is now a wild garden.

<Downloads version="1.7.0" />

## What changed

- The Garden workshop, under a glass roof, replaces the Cozy living room.
- In the workshop a working friend sits at a potting table and waits by the bell for your approval.
- Without an agent, friends in the workshop sit down to read, have tea or rest, then stroll on.
- A friend shows its agent's state: it stays put, comes forward to ask, then goes back.
- The agent's state dot sits in the name tag, beside the friend's name.
- New pictures for the outdoor places, with the same calm lawn in every season.
- The Stone courtyard is now the Wild garden: an open meadow with a pond and a big oak.
- Friends, flowers, trees and gifts go wherever the picture shows ground, up to the doors.
- Pick a season and a fitting scene to call up its festival on any day.
- Baubles sit on the Christmas tree's branches, and gifts on the ground cast a soft shadow.
- At night, gifts and plants take on the night's blue, and a lantern lights the ground.
- Fireflies are fewer and softer, mostly a gentle glow.
- A friend holds the bug net and the fishing rod in front of it.
- The fishing float sits in the water with a ripple.
- On a harvest moon night the moon is whole and round.
- A flower or a tree you drag stays under the pointer and stops swaying while you hold it.
- Dragging a planted tree stays smooth in a busy garden.
