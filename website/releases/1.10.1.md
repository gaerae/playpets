# 1.10.1

<ReleaseMeta />

Six flowers now bloom as whole patches, and a click on a flower, a tree or a gift gets a little answer back. Things sent to the desktop arrive with a hop, and friends mind the furniture.

<Downloads version="1.10.1" />

## What changed

- Tulips, cosmos, sunflowers, lavender, hydrangeas and camellias bloom as a patch outdoors.
- Right-click one for **One bloom** or **A patch**, in the yard and on the desktop.
- A clicked flower bobs and drops petals; a tree rustles and lets leaves or snow go.
- A clicked gift bobs, and now and then a friend nearby says something about it.
- On the desktop, a friend sent out drops in and waves, and a gift or a flower pops up.
- Planting a tree shows its sapling on soil where it would go, greyed where it can't.
- A friend walking toward you grows steadily, without shrinking on the way.
- In the garden workshop, friends walk around the tables and reach a desk from behind.
- In a small window, the ring a click on the grass leaves is as small as the friends.
- In a small window, the chat is narrower and only as tall as the conversation.
- <Only os="Windows" /> Opening **Play** no longer shows a sideways scrollbar.
- Album cards are the same height in every row, and arrow keys and Enter choose them.
- Desktop friends stay on the grass instead of drifting up above it.
- The desktop follows a screen whose Dock, taskbar or resolution changes.
- Pulling a grass pebble keeps going over the Dock or the taskbar, and a let-go never sticks.
- A flower on the desktop stays where you left it when it grows while PlayPets is closed.
- A save that fails tries again at once, and the notice blames the disk only when it is full.
