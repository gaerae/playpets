# 1.7.2

<ReleaseMeta />

Show on desktop answers right away, and the yard stays smooth while friends talk, coding agents work and things are saved.

<Downloads version="1.7.2" />

## What changed

- Show on desktop answers at once, also the first time and right after everything came back in.
- The yard opens sooner.
- The yard stays smooth while friends talk, coding agents report and the pointer moves.
- A long conversation opens on its latest lines; earlier ones come in as you scroll up.
- Friends keep walking smoothly when a Windows laptop runs on battery.
- The backdrop and weather stay sharp when the window moves to a screen of another resolution.
- Patting, picking a scene, moving things and the tray's switches no longer wait for saving.
- With many friends, the yard no longer stutters every ten minutes.

Sending friends and gifts out is in [Desktop mode](../guide/desktop.md).
