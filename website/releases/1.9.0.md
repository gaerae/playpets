# 1.9.0

<ReleaseMeta />

What you found no longer stays in the album: set a flower, a tree, a fish or a keepsake out again, in the yard or on the desktop, where it answers a click.

<Downloads version="1.9.0" />

## What changed

- Anything you found can come out of its album card again, into the yard or onto the desktop.
- Finds on the desktop answer a click: fish swim, butterflies flap, keepsakes sparkle.
- Desktop gifts come alive: lanterns light at evening, pinwheels turn, wind chimes swing.
- A friend who strolls to a bench, swing or picnic blanket stands on it.
- The wind chime, the hanging pot and the mobile hang from the top of a screen.
- Eighteen new album entries, now 203: eight flowers, four trees, four keepsakes and two gifts.
- Seven keepsakes are redrawn, and the app carries 1.3 MB less keepsake art.
- The winter lights start on November 15, and the year-end letter comes from December 15.
- Wide keepsakes stand on the ground instead of hovering above it.

The album is in [The yard and games](../guide/play.md), and the desktop in [Desktop mode](../guide/desktop.md).
