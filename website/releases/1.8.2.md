# 1.8.2

<ReleaseMeta />

Gifts, flowers and trees on the desktop stay right where you set them down, high or low, just as friends do.

<Downloads version="1.8.2" />

## What changed

- Gifts, flowers and trees on the desktop stay where you put them, not only along the bottom.
- The first time out, they still start along the bottom of the screen.
- Friends and things on the desktop come back onto a screen when one is unplugged or resized.
- A tree that grows near the edge of the screen stays on it.

Sending gifts, flowers and trees out is in [Desktop mode](../guide/desktop.md).
