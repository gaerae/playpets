# 1.0.0

<ReleaseMeta />

PlayPets 1.0.0 keeps a small companion window focused on scenery and friends, with brief play when you choose it.

<Downloads version="1.0.0" />

## What changed

- Small windows open a short play strip from a friend. Place, Play and Menu stay distinct in larger windows.
- Ask a friend to dig, find eight keepsakes, and plant cherry, maple or pine trees that grow while the app is closed. The collection guide explains the odds without daily tasks.
- Larger furniture fits the friends, and menus use tighter spacing with consistent section boundaries.
- Update details stay over the existing yard and can be collapsed while downloading; installation and restart remain explicit.
- Background creation kits preserve whether a place has usable soil.
- The app version is 1.0.0; MSIX uses the required fourth component, 1.0.0.0. MSIX updates stay package-managed, and GitHub installers keep the signed updater.

Microsoft Store certification and publication are separate from this GitHub release. Store availability is not implied by this page.
