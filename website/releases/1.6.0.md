# 1.6.0

<ReleaseMeta />

Winter comes to the yard: roll a snowman together, follow footprints in the snow, trim the Christmas tree and read a letter from your closest friend. Then spring shoots and cherry blossoms.

<Downloads version="1.6.0" />

## What changed

- In a winter yard, roll snowballs with your friends into a snowman, then give it a hat and a scarf.
- Animal footprints cross the snow; follow them with a friend to a small find in a bush.
- In the winter lights, now December 1 to 31, friends bring a Christmas tree, a reindeer and Santa.
- Hang up to three album finds a day on the Christmas tree.
- Snowy December nights grow the tree grand; with reindeer and Santa out, a sleigh crosses the moon.
- From December 26, your closest friend writes you a year-end letter, and you can write back.
- In spring, shoots come up after rain; a friend digs them up for the album's new book.
- At the cherry blossom festival, friends gather to view the flowers. Catch a petal for a wish.
- The album holds 169 finds in eight books, with the Footprints and Spring shoots books.
- Fireflies, the gudgeon, winter trees and other look-alikes are now easy to tell apart.
- Claude Code and Codex CLI sign in from PlayPets, with no terminal.
- **Report an AI reply** in About sends a short public form, without your conversation.
- Friends keep every AI reply suitable for ages 13 and up.
- A butterfly or dragonfly in front of a friend or a gift can be caught.
- Fishing goes on when you resize the window.
- The yard rests while its window is hidden, and downloads are smaller.
- <Only os="Windows" /> The Store version checks the Store for a newer version and shows it in the app.
- <Only os="Windows" /> Without WebView2, PlayPets offers its download page instead of closing.
- <Only os="Windows" /> The Store version needs Windows 10 version 2004 or later, and ARM PCs get the ARM64 build.
- <Only os="macOS" /> An update from a copy opened in the disk image asks you to move PlayPets to Applications first.

## Also

- The [front page](../index.md) now shows every album book, every seasonal event and what each closeness level brings.
- Before uninstalling, disconnect coding agents and delete AI connections; see [Install](../guide/install.md).

The winter and spring additions are in [The yard and games](../guide/play.md); signing in is in [Chat with a friend](../guide/chat.md).
